Suggested Classroom Use
  1. Start at New Moon and ask students where the illuminated half is.
  2. Move slowly toward First Quarter and ask why the Moon appears half lit even though half of the whole Moon is always illuminated.
  3. Stop at Full Moon and compare this geometry with lunar-eclipse geometry.
  4. Have students explain why Earth's shadow is not responsible for ordinary lunar phases.
  5. Use the slider as evidence for the Johnny/Suzie writing prompt.
